# Delete Your Mixroom Account

Last updated: 2026-03-16

This page explains how to delete your Mixroom account and what happens to your
data when you do.

Operator:

- (주)믹스룸 (Mixroom)
- Privacy contact: privacy@mixroom.ai

## 1. How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

1. In the Mixroom app:
   Open `Legal & Privacy Center` and choose `Delete account`.
2. On the web:
   Email `privacy@mixroom.ai` with the subject line `Delete account request`
   from the email address associated with your Mixroom account.

If needed to verify your identity, we may ask for additional information before
processing the request.

## 2. Important billing note

If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it first in the App Store,
Google Play, or any applicable web billing portal. Deleting your Mixroom
account does not itself cancel store or third-party billing.

## 3. What we delete

When your deletion request is completed, Mixroom will delete or de-identify
Mixroom-side account data that is no longer needed to provide the service or
meet legal obligations. This can include:

- your Mixroom profile and account metadata;
- handle, bio, avatar URL, and related profile fields;
- Mixroom-side account records associated with your user ID;
- pending in-service user profile state that is no longer required.

## 4. What we may retain

We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for:

- subscription, billing, tax, accounting, reconciliation, and fraud-prevention
  records;
- security logging, abuse prevention, and incident investigation;
- compliance with legal obligations, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our
  agreements;
- short-lived backups and disaster-recovery copies that are overwritten or
  deleted in the ordinary course.

## 5. Device data

Deleting your Mixroom account does not automatically remove local project files,
exports, media, or cached data stored on your device or in your personal cloud
backups. You should also delete those from your device if you want them removed.

## 6. Timing

We will process deletion requests within a commercially reasonable period and
within any deadline required by applicable law.

## 7. Contact

For deletion questions, contact `privacy@mixroom.ai`.
