or "our") collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you use the

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mixroom mobile app, desktop app, websites,
account services, AI features, billing flows, and support interactions that
link to or reference this policy.

Additional privacy resources:

- Account deletion and retention details: `https://mixroom.ai/delete-account`
- Current subprocessors: `https://mixroom.ai/subprocessors`

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information from you, your device, and service providers you choose
to use with Mixroom.

### A. Information you provide directly

Depending on the feature you use, this may include:

- account credentials and identifiers, such as email address, username/handle,
  password, and account ID;
- profile information, such as display name, first name, last name, bio, and
  avatar URL;
- age-related signup information, including birthdate or a minimum-age check;
- communications with us, including support, privacy, and account requests;
- marketing preferences, such as newsletter opt-in status;
- content you choose to submit through the Services, such as prompts, support
  messages, or metadata you add to uploads.

### B. Information collected automatically

When you use Mixroom, we may collect:

- network and request information, such as IP address, approximate region
  derived from IP, request timestamps, referrer URL, and user agent;
- device and app information, such as app version, platform, locale,
  environment, and basic diagnostic data;
- analytics identifiers, such as device ID, distinct ID, and session ID;
- crash, error, and performance data if analytics and diagnostics are enabled;
- usage events, such as screen views, feature use, first-project creation, and
  purchase flow events;
- save-time project telemetry, including project JSON, plugin/effect settings,
  and project-structure snapshots; if optional analytics and diagnostics are
  enabled, those uploads can also include associated assistant chat history;
- security and operational logs, such as API request metadata, authentication
  attempts, rate-limit events, and service health events.

### C. Cookies and similar web technologies

When you use Mixroom websites or web-based flows, Mixroom and service providers
may use cookies, local storage, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to:

- keep you signed in and remember settings;
- measure traffic, product usage, and campaign effectiveness;
- maintain security, detect abuse, and help prevent fraud;
- support core site functionality.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but blocking certain
technologies may affect site functionality.

### D. AI and project-context data

If you use Mixroom's cloud AI features, Mixroom may send a structured request
to our backend systems and model providers. That request can include:

- your prompt and limited recent chat history;
- project summary data, such as BPM, clip file names, track gain/pan, effect
  names, selected items, automation target names and IDs, and lightweight audio
  summary statistics;
- optional request metadata such as project ID and AI feature name;
- optional client analytics context if analytics is enabled.

For Mixroom's current chat-driven cloud AI flow, raw audio files, rendered
stems, waveform/sample buffers, the full project file, and plugin state blobs
are not sent through that cloud chat path.

Mixroom's backend is designed to support multiple alternative cloud AI
providers. Only the provider that Mixroom has actively enabled for the relevant
production deployment will process a given cloud AI request.

### E. Media and device-permission data

If you choose to use media-related features, Mixroom may access:

- microphone input for recording;
- photos, videos, audio files, and file-system selections that you choose to
  import, edit, export, save, or upload;
- saved exports if you ask Mixroom to write media back to your device or photo
  library.

Local projects and media generally remain on your device unless you choose to
share, export, upload, or connect them to an online feature.

### F. Billing and transaction data

If paid plans are offered and you purchase one, we and our payment-related
providers may process:

- subscription tier, provider, and entitlement status;
- store transaction or purchase verification data;
- product ID, billing cycle, price, currency, and restore or management data;
- limited records needed for fraud prevention, reconciliation, support, tax,
  accounting, and compliance.

### G. Social sign-in and third-party account data

If you sign in or connect a third-party service, we may receive information
from that provider, such as your account identifier, email address, display
name, and verification status, subject to that provider's own settings and
policies.

### H. Optional third-party upload data

If you use optional platform-upload features, Mixroom may send the files and
metadata you choose to the destination platform. For example, if you use a
YouTube upload feature, Google/YouTube may receive the selected video file,
thumbnail, title, description, privacy setting, and related OAuth credentials
needed to perform the upload.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, maintain, and secure your account;
- provide editing, recording, export, sharing, AI, billing, and support
  features;
- operate and improve Mixroom's audio, video, AI, analytics, and account
  systems;
- personalize product experience where you have enabled relevant settings;
- process purchases, verify entitlements, restore subscriptions, and manage
  billing support;
- prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, automated misuse, excessive usage,
  and violations of our terms, including through logs, rate limits, and
  related protective controls;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements;
- send service messages and, where permitted, product emails you request or
  opt into.

## 4. Legal Bases and Regional Rights

Where required by applicable law, we rely on one or more of the following legal
bases:

- performance of a contract with you;
- your consent;
- compliance with legal obligations;
- our legitimate interests in operating, securing, improving, and supporting
  the Services.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete,
export, or object to certain processing of your personal data, or to withdraw
consent where consent is the basis for processing.

If you are in a jurisdiction that gives you a right to appeal a privacy
decision or to authorize an agent to make a request on your behalf, contact us
and we will explain the available process.

## 5. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

- with service providers that help us operate Mixroom, such as cloud hosting,
  authentication, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, billing, and
  security vendors;
- with AI model and infrastructure providers when you use cloud AI features;
- with app stores, payment processors, and billing partners to process,
  verify, restore, reconcile, and manage subscriptions;
- with identity providers when you choose social sign-in or linked-account
  features;
- with third-party platforms when you ask us to upload, share, or transfer your
  content to them;
- if required by law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process;
- in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, sale of
  assets, or similar transaction;
- with your direction or consent.

## 6. Current Categories of Processors and Third Parties

Depending on Mixroom's deployment configuration and the features you use,
Mixroom's service providers or integrated platforms may include:

- Amazon Web Services, including Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB,
  Secrets Manager, and CloudWatch;
- PostHog for product analytics;
- Sentry for crash and error reporting;
- Apple and Google for app distribution, in-app purchases, purchase
  verification, and subscription management;
- OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google model services, depending on which cloud AI
  provider Mixroom has actively configured for the backend handling your
  request;
- Google, Apple, and Kakao for sign-in flows;
- Google/YouTube if you choose an upload-to-YouTube workflow.

Third-party providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies
in addition to ours.

If you want to know which cloud AI provider is currently active for your
deployment or request path, contact `privacy@mixroom.ai`.

## 7. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Marketing Choices

You can control certain privacy settings in Mixroom, including:

- enabling or disabling usage analytics and crash diagnostics;
- enabling or disabling whether optional analytics and diagnostics also include
  assistant chat history in save-time project telemetry uploads;
- requesting export, correction, or deletion of personal data;
- deleting your Mixroom account;
- managing product email preferences where offered.

Disabling analytics and crash diagnostics does not disable data processing that
is necessary to provide core account, security, subscription, or service
functionality.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes
described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the Services, maintain
security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal obligations, and
support accounting and tax requirements.

Examples include:

- account and profile data, until deletion or longer where needed for legal,
  security, or backup purposes;
- billing and entitlement records, as needed for reconciliation, fraud
  prevention, accounting, tax, and support;
- support and privacy request records, as needed to respond and document the
  request;
- analytics, AI usage, and server, access, and security logs, for limited
  periods appropriate to product operations, security, abuse prevention, and
  debugging;
- device-local data, until you delete it, clear it, or uninstall the app,
  subject to your operating system and backup settings.

## 9. International Transfers

Mixroom and its providers may process information in countries other than the
country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection
rules. Where required, we take steps intended to protect personal data during
cross-border transfers.

## 10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to
protect personal data. These measures may include access controls, encryption
where appropriate, logging and monitoring, fraud and abuse detection, request
validation, and automated protections such as rate limiting. No method of
transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Mixroom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not intend for
children under 13 to create accounts or use the Services without a legally
valid basis to do so. If we learn that we collected personal data from a child
under 13 in a way that violates applicable law, we will take steps to delete
that information.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal data, contact
privacy@mixroom.ai.

## 12. Your Requests

To submit a privacy request, contact privacy@mixroom.ai. You can ask us to:

- provide a copy of certain personal data;
- correct inaccurate profile information;
- delete your Mixroom account and associated Mixroom-side records;
- explain certain categories of data processing.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. If you have an
active paid subscription, cancel it first through the App Store, Google Play,
or any applicable web billing portal before requesting account deletion, since
deleting your Mixroom account does not itself cancel store billing.

We also publish a public deletion-request page at
`https://mixroom.ai/delete-account` so users can initiate deletion without
opening the app.
